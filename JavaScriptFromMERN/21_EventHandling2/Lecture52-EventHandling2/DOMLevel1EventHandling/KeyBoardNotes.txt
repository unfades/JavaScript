onkeydown() onkeypress()

whenever a key is pressed on your keyboard,
onkeydown() is gonna run

onkeypress() works only on when you press
a key with an ascii, not for function keys, shift etc

onkeypress() is DEPRECATED do not use it anymore.

it may be dropped at any time by modern browsers.
technically it is irrelevant, onkeydown() can handle everything onkeypress() can.
