CDNs we used are bootstrap and fontawesome.

cdns are content delivery networks, which is like servers near us
which provide us content they have.
kinda like netflix has servers near us to serve us videos.
these cdns are for styling and prettyness.

CDN is the better way rather than downloading the css file,
because we can change it up by changing the class,
dont have to host the css on our own servers...

also bootstrap makes our page responsive based on the device
