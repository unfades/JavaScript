The style property itself has child properties which are used to set/get
specific styles.

Like: 1 backgroundColor,
2 color,
3 borderColor,
4 fontFamily,
5 fontWeight bold, bolder, lighter, normal, 100 to 900,
6 fontStyle normal, italic, oblique.
