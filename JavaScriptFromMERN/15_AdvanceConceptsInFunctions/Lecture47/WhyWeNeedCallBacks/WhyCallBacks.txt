In JS Programming theres 2 types of programs

1 Synchronous where it runs step by step 


example: A ticket for a train is being purchased by a guy, the rest are standing in line
waiting their turn. That's synchronous mode. 

2 Asynchronous where it does multiple activities at the same time.

In a restaurant, there's multiple people being served at the same time. Multiple actions.
That's asynchronous mode. If a restaurant was synchronous, people wouldn't go there.

one thing at a time is synchronous, multiple things at once is asynchronous.

AJAX uses callbacks a lot. JS does not have multithreading, it all runs on one thread.
Callbacks are most helpful in Asynchronous programming. That's where they're used.
